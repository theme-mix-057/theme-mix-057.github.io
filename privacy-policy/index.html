ThemeArt: Themes Widgets Icons

Privacy Policy · Effective Date: October 5, 2026

ThemeArt: Themes Widgets Icons ("ThemeArt", "we", "our", or "us") is a phone customization application that helps users personalize their devices with themes, stylish widgets, custom app icons, icon packs, and customizable Control Center styles.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use ThemeArt. We aim to collect and use information only as needed to provide, maintain, secure, and improve the features you choose to use.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, ThemeArt may process certain information from your device.

🖼️ Photos & Media Images selected by you for wallpapers, customization, or other requested features.
📱 Device Information Basic technical information needed for compatibility, diagnostics, and app functionality.
⚙️ App Usage Information Information about interactions with features may be processed to improve the application.
🛡️ Accessibility Service Accessibility access may be used for Control Center and supported system-control features.

2. Photos and Media Access

ThemeArt may request access to photos, images, or media stored on your device when a feature requires it.

Images that you select are used to provide the feature you explicitly request, such as selecting an image for customization or using an image in a supported personalization workflow.

ThemeArt does not need access to every photo on your device simply for you to browse or use the application's general customization features. Where the operating system provides a more limited photo-selection mechanism, ThemeArt may use the access granted through that mechanism.

✨ Images selected by you are used for the feature you choose to use. ThemeArt does not sell your personal photos to third parties.

3. Themes, Widgets, Icons, and Control Styles

ThemeArt provides themes, widgets, custom app icons, icon packs, and Control Center styles for personalizing your device. When you browse or save content, the application may process information necessary to display, download, save, or apply that content.

Favorite items, saved designs, and customization preferences may be stored locally on your device or through services required to provide the relevant feature, depending on the implementation of the application.

ThemeArt may also process basic information about the content you choose to view or use in order to provide the requested customization experience.

4. Accessibility Service

ThemeArt may use the Android Accessibility Service API to provide specific user-requested features, including supported Control Center functionality and system-control features.

Accessibility Service access may be used to support functionality such as:

  • Providing the supported Control feature when enabled by the user
  • Supporting requested system-control actions
  • Supporting media or volume controls where required by the enabled Control feature
  • Providing other functionality explicitly requested by the user through the supported Control feature

Accessibility access is not used to read, collect, store, or share personal or sensitive user information unrelated to the requested functionality.

🛡️ ThemeArt uses Accessibility Service access only to enable the user-requested customization and supported system-control features described above.
